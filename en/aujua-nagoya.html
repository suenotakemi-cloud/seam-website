Dropping in while you are in Nagoya

Sakae, home to Parco and Matsuzakaya, is Nagoya's foremost shopping district. SEAM stands on the street right by Yabacho Station, across the ground and second floors.

It is easy to fold into your route home after shopping in Sakae.

Pick up the bottles and choose salon-exclusive hair care for yourself. No treatment required, and you are welcome to come just to shop. If you would like member prices, you can sign up in store.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit just to buy Aujua in Nagoya?
Yes. At SEAM NAGOYA you can buy Aujua without any treatment or booking. Shopping-only visits are welcome.
How do I get to the Nagoya store?
Right by Yabacho Station (Sakae area, ground and second floors).
Do I need a booking?
No booking needed. Right by Yabacho Station (Sakae area, ground and second floors) — just come during opening hours.
Can I buy without being a member?
Anyone can buy in store. Membership applies only to the online shop, and registration is done in store.

Stock changes over time. To be sure, please check with SEAM NAGOYA on Instagram or in store.
