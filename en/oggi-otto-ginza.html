Dropping in while you are in Ginza

Ginza is one of Japan's foremost shopping districts, lined with department stores and luxury brands. With many salons and cosmetics shops at street level, it suits anyone who likes to compare hair care before choosing.

SEAM GINZA is one minute from Ginza-itchome Station, Exit 7. It is also walkable from Ginza and Kyobashi Stations, and close to the department stores such as Matsuya and Ginza Mitsukoshi.

No treatment, no booking. While you are in Ginza to shop or dine, you can simply come and buy salon-exclusive hair care. If you would like to keep buying at member prices, you can sign up on the spot in store.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit just to buy Oggi otto in Ginza?
Yes. At SEAM GINZA you can buy Oggi otto without any treatment or booking. Shopping-only visits are welcome.
How do I get to the Ginza store?
1 min walk from Exit 7, Ginza-itchome Station (also walkable from Ginza and Kyobashi Stations).
Do I need a booking?
No booking needed. 1 min walk from Exit 7, Ginza-itchome Station (also walkable from Ginza and Kyobashi Stations). Just drop in during opening hours.
Can I buy without being a member?
Anyone can buy in store. Membership applies only to the online shop, and registration is done in store.
Do you have Oggi otto in stock?
Stock changes over time. To be sure, please check with SEAM GINZA on Instagram or in store.
