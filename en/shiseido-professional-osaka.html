Dropping in while you are in Osaka

Minami-Horie, lined with select shops and cafés, is the stylish corner of Osaka's Minami. It is within walking distance of Amerikamura, Shinsaibashi and Namba.

SEAM is a 2-minute walk from Exit 6 of Yotsubashi Station, easy to visit between shopping and café stops in Horie.

No treatment or booking required. Even when you want to restock your whole home-care routine, you can simply come and buy salon-exclusive products. Membership sign-up is handled in store.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit just to buy Shiseido Professional in Osaka?
Yes. At SEAM OSAKA HORIE you can buy Shiseido Professional without any treatment or booking. Shopping-only visits are welcome.
How do I get to the Osaka store?
2 min walk from Exit 6, Yotsubashi Station (Minami-Horie).
Do I need a booking?
No booking needed. 2 min walk from Exit 6, Yotsubashi Station (Minami-Horie). Just drop in during opening hours.
Can I buy without being a member?
Anyone can buy in store. Membership applies only to the online shop, and registration is done in store.

Stock changes over time. To be sure, please check with SEAM OSAKA HORIE on Instagram or in store.
