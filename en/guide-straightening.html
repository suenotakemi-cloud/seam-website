Shampoo and care
after straightening

Hair that chose straight has borrowed the power of heat and chemicals, and inside it is delicate
The key to keeping straightening beautiful for long is, in fact, daily home care

What happens in straightened hair

Straightening unbinds the connections inside the hair with chemicals and resets it straight with heat
The shape may be stable, but inside it dries easily and stays delicate for a while
The ends especially are where past straightening has piled up — the place that most deserves care

Small hints for every day

  • · On the day of the treatment, follow your stylist's guidance on washing and tight ties
  • · Choose a shampoo that washes gently and keeps the moisture in
  • · Treatment mostly on the ends. If flat roots bother you, keep it off the roots
  • · Dry it before bed. Damp hair rubbing against a pillow is the worst friction of all
  • · On days you use an iron, take a moment first with a heat-protecting milk or mist

"Hair improvement" and straightening

Acid straightening and what is called hair improvement belong to the same family, in that they use chemicals and heat
Which treatments you have had changes the home care that suits you
A vague name is fine — the finder asks how often, not what it was called

Frequently asked questions

Can I shampoo on the day I have straightening?
It depends on the product and the menu. Your stylist's instructions are the surest guide. If you are unsure, holding off that day and washing gently from the next is said to be the safe choice
What shampoo should I choose after straightening?
Something that washes gently and keeps moisture in tends to suit. If bleach and colour overlap, the choice changes, so we suggest a finder that includes your history
Should I care for the straightened lengths and new roots differently?
It is natural for roots and ends to be in different states. The basic approach is treatment mostly on the ends, keeping the roots airy
