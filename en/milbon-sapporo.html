Dropping in while you are in Sapporo

Odori, the heart of Sapporo, sits beside Odori Park and the Tanukikoji shopping street. It is easy to reach through the underground walkway, so the weather need not stop you.

SEAM is one minute from Odori subway station, within walking distance of Susukino — easy to fold into a day in the city centre.

In Hokkaido, where the temperature swings and the air is dry, it helps to keep moisturising and repairing home care in stock. You can top up on salon-exclusive products without any treatment.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit just to buy Milbon in Sapporo?
Yes. At SEAM SAPPORO you can buy Milbon without any treatment or booking. Shopping-only visits are welcome.
How do I get to the Sapporo store?
1 min walk from Odori Subway Station.
Do I need a booking?
No booking needed. 1 min walk from Odori Subway Station — just come during opening hours.
Can I buy without being a member?
Anyone can buy in store. Membership applies only to the online shop, and registration is done in store.
Do you have Milbon in stock?
Stock changes over time. To be sure, please check with SEAM SAPPORO on Instagram or in store.
