Dropping in while you are in Fukuoka

Daimyo, next to Tenjin, is where Fukuoka's trends start. Select shops and cafés line the streets, so it is easy to drop in between errands.

SEAM is five minutes from Nishitetsu Tenjin Station, in a corner of Daimyo within walking distance of Tenjin's department stores.

Leave-in treatments, styling products — you can try what you have been curious about and choose in person. No booking needed, and shopping-only visits are welcome.
